About Calb

Calb is a Canadian-born DJ based in Oslo. He has been mixing music and making mashups since 2013, and DJing professionally since 2021, playing house, disco, garage and groove at clubs, bars, restaurants and private events.

His longform mashup albums have over 4.5 million views on YouTube and have been covered by NME, MusicRadar, Okayplayer and EDM.com.

He loves Bon Iver, Kanye West and Daft Punk.
