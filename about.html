About

Calb is based in Oslo, Norway, but was born in Canada in 1995. He's been mixing music and making dumb mashups since 2013. He has also made some different music projects with friends, but those are secret. Calb is a student, he works as a barista, and he really wants you to hire him as a DJ. He loves Bon Iver, Kanye West, Daft Punk, and more. His handle on Instagram is @calbmusic.
