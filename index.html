Visual Mix Projects

Calb's visual mashup albums have been called "interdisciplinary works of art" and "compelling composites that transform the beloved source material into something new entirely". Calb has used music from Daft Punk, Michael Jackson, Bon Iver, Frank Ocean, Tame Impala, The 1975, James Blake, and A$AP Rocky to make these full-length visual mixes. He's currently working on a project using music from Coldplay's discography.

DJ Mixes

As a DJ since 2013, Calb has developed a distinct musical taste and mixing style. These mixes showcase that diversity of genres and styles, with low-key vibes as shown in "Triple Birthday Deluxe - Opening Set" and bangers for the peak hour with "Totally Unprepared Club Stuff".
