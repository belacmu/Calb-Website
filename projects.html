Longform Mashup Projects

Calb's visual mashup albums have been called "interdisciplinary works of art" and "compelling composites that transform beloved source material". Calb has used music from Daft Punk, Beyonce, Michael Jackson, Bon Iver, Frank Ocean, Tame Impala, The 1975, James Blake, and A$AP Rocky to make these full-length visual mixes.
